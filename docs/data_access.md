# Data access

The repository includes approximately 3% of the surrogate dataset and one JLU control plus one JLU patient for demonstration. The remaining clinical recordings are available through a direct request to the data contact:

**[hx.yang2@siat.ac.cn](mailto:hx.yang2@siat.ac.cn)**

Please describe your institution, intended research use, and the dataset or materials needed. The contact will provide the applicable availability conditions and access arrangements. The repository does not promise unrestricted access to the complete clinical dataset.

Suggested manuscript data-availability statement:

> Analysis code, frozen model weights, a small surrogate dataset, and two curated clinical examples accompany the repository. Requests for access to the complete clinical data should be directed to hx.yang2@siat.ac.cn and are subject to the applicable data-access conditions.

The original source ABP dataset is publicly available from [UCI: Cuff-Less Blood Pressure Estimation](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/340/cuff), DOI 10.24432/C5B602, as cited in manuscript reference 23. For the complete derived surrogate arrays and the fixed study split, use the same contact. The MIT license applies to repository software; it does not override rights or access conditions associated with source recordings or clinical data.
